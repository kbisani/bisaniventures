Dosawalla

Our first game: a cooking game about one cook’s road from a village street cart to the grandest restaurant in the city.

  • Pour, spread and flip crispy dosas, made to order
  • 80 stops across four places to cook in
  • A story told in painted scenes, with regulars who come back
  • Plays offline, with no account and nothing collected
